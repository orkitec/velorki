• An easier first start: Velorki asks for your location, opens the map over your country with bike lanes and stops shown, and points out where to begin.
• Ride straight away: Ride beside Save starts recording and guides you along the route you planned.
• Add a stop during a ride: tap a café, shop or water point on the map and add it to your route. Guidance carries on while the route is worked out again.
• More on the map: train stations, bus, tram and ferry stops, pharmacies, hospitals, picnic sites, viewpoints, beaches and inner-tube vending machines. Search "bus", "train" or "ferry" to find the nearest.
• Downloads happen right on the map: the area, the search and the data a route needs, with progress and a way to cancel.
• Map data is updated only when something in your area has really changed, and a note over the map tells you.
• A tap on the map while searching just closes the search.
• Imprint in Settings → About.
