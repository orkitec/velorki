Velorki needs no account and no sign-in; every screen is reachable right away.

Velorki Plus (monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions, with a free trial) is bought in the app through the App Store:
- Open the Settings tab, scroll to the "Velorki Plus" row and tap it. The paywall lists what Plus includes, the price and period of each package, the trial terms, the auto-renewal terms, "Restore purchases", and links to the Terms of use and the Privacy policy.
- The paywall also opens from any Plus feature: for example, on a route's page in the Library tab, "Share link".
"Restore purchases" is on the paywall and under the "Velorki Plus" row in Settings, and works without any login.

Everything else is free and works without Plus: planning, navigation, recording, the library, offline regions, sensors, and GPX/FIT import and export.

Location: the app asks for "When In Use" location to show you on the map and to record a ride. Background location runs only while a ride is being recorded or navigated, with the blue indicator shown. Apple Health, Bluetooth and notifications are asked for only when the matching switch or screen is used.

To test routing without travelling: in the Plan tab, tap two points on the map; the route is computed right away.
