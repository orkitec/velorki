Velorki plans bike routes, builds loops and records your rides. It runs on
OpenStreetMap data with bike-specific routing, and the whole app is open
source.

PLANNING
• Tap waypoints on the map and get a route for the bike you are on: trekking,
  road, gravel or mountain bike.
• Alternatives for every route, an elevation profile, and a breakdown of the
  surfaces you will ride on.
• Loops: say how far you want to ride and, if you like, one place to pass, and
  Velorki proposes round trips that come back to where you started.
• Search for places, save routes to your library, and download map regions to
  use without a connection.

RECORDING
• Records with the screen off and keeps recording if the app is closed.
• Distance, time, speed and ascent while you ride, with auto-pause and a
  notification that shows the ride in progress.
• Follow a planned route, then review and edit the ride afterwards.

FILES
• Open GPX and FIT files from your files app, from mail, from a browser or
  through the share sheet.
• Export routes and rides as GPX or FIT and hand them to Komoot, Garmin
  Connect, Strava or anything else that reads those formats.

FREE, AND VELORKI PLUS
Everything that runs on the phone is free: planning, loops, recording, the
library, offline map regions, and GPX and FIT import and export.

Velorki Plus is a subscription for the parts that need a server or an account
with another service:
• Strava: upload a recorded ride, import your Strava routes.
• Ride with GPS: routes and trips, both directions.
• The assistant: describe the ride you want in a sentence and get a route.
• Share links for routes and rides.

Velorki Plus is offered with a free trial and renews until you cancel it in
Google Play. Terms: https://velorki.com/terms

PRIVACY
There is no account and no sign-up. Routes, rides and settings stay on your
phone. Nothing goes to Strava or Ride with GPS until you connect the account
and ask for it. The assistant asks for your consent before any text leaves the
device, and the position it sends is rounded to about a kilometre.
Privacy policy: https://velorki.com/privacy
