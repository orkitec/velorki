Velorki plans bike routes, guides you along them and records your rides, all on your phone and offline too: planning, routing, search, navigation and recording. A free GPS bike navigation app, route planner, ride tracker and bike computer in one. No account, no sign-up.

Your routes and rides stay with you: on your phone, or synced through your own Google Drive, never through our servers. The code is public, so anyone can check that. Export them as GPX, FIT or TCX anytime.

PLANNING
• Tap waypoints on the map and get a route for the bike you are on: touring, road, gravel, mountain bike, or the direct way.
• Loops from your door: say how far you want to ride and, if you like, one place to pass, and Velorki proposes round trips that come back to where you started.
• Alternative routes, an elevation profile and a breakdown of the surfaces you will ride on.
• Colour the route by gradient, surface, wind or rain.
• Search places offline, save routes to your library, and download a region once to plan, search and navigate there without a connection.
• Plan in the browser at app.velorki.com, signed in to the same Google account: your library is there, and Plus if you have it.

MAP AND WEATHER
• A cycle map drawn from the routing data, offline: bike paths and lanes, one-way streets, steps and climbs.
• Stops on the map: water, cafés, bakeries, toilets, bike repair and more, and the ones along the route ahead.
• Rain radar, wind and clouds on the map, with a time slider into the next hours.
• The wind and rain along your route for the time you leave.

NAVIGATION
• Turn-by-turn directions with spoken cues, also with the screen locked, also for routes you import.
• Leave the route and choose what happens: a way back to it, a new route to the destination, or nothing at all.

RECORDING
• Records with the screen off and keeps recording if the app is closed, with auto-pause and a notification that shows the ride.
• Choose the figures you see while riding: distance, speed, time, ascent, heart rate, cadence, power, what is left of the route.
• Heart rate, speed, cadence and power from Bluetooth sensors.
• Each ride gets a page with its map, charts and splits.

FILES AND CONNECTIONS
• Open GPX, FIT and TCX files from your files app, mail, a browser or the share sheet; export routes and rides as GPX, FIT or TCX.
• Connect two popular route and ride platforms to upload your rides and bring your routes in. Free.

FREE, AND VELORKI PLUS
Everything the phone can do on its own is free, and so is sync.

Velorki Plus is a subscription for what needs our servers:
• Finer weather: radar across Europe from the national weather services, and a sharper forecast along the route.
• The assistant: describe the ride you want in a sentence and get a route, or ask it about the route on the map.
• Share links: turn a route or a ride into a link anyone can open in a browser.

Velorki Plus is offered with a free trial on the yearly plan and renews until you cancel it in Google Play.

PRIVACY
No ads, no analytics. Nothing goes to another platform until you connect the account and ask for it. The assistant asks for your consent before any text leaves the device, and the position it sends is rounded to about a kilometre.
