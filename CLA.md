you keep it, and you may use your own contribution however you like.

## How to sign

You do not send anything by post, and you do not edit this file.

Open a pull request. A bot comments on it with the exact sentence to sign, you
post that sentence as a comment on the pull request, and the CLA check turns
green. Your signature — your GitHub username, the pull request and the date —
is stored in this repository on the `cla-signatures` branch. One signature
covers all your later pull requests.
