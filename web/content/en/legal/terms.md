## 2. What Velorki is, and what it is not

Velorki plans bicycle routes, generates loops, searches places, guides you with
turn instructions and records rides. It is built on OpenStreetMap data.

Velorki is a planning aid, not a safety system. Map data, routing data,
elevation data and place data come from open sources and can be out of date,
incomplete or wrong. A route may cross a closed road, a private track, a ford,
a path that is not legal or not safe to cycle, or a road that has changed since
the data was collected.

**You are responsible for how you ride.** Watch the road, obey traffic law and
signs, judge for yourself whether a road or a path is safe and permitted, and
do not operate the phone while riding in a way that endangers you or others. Do
not rely on Velorki in situations where a wrong turn or a missing road would be
dangerous, and take a fallback (a paper map, a charged phone, a known way home)
on remote rides. Ride recording and navigation depend on your phone's GPS,
battery, storage and operating system, none of which we control.

## 6. Your content and share links

Your routes, your rides and your files stay yours. We claim no ownership of
them. They stay on your phone unless you send them somewhere yourself.

When you create a **share link** (a Plus feature) the route or ride you shared,
with its track, its name and its statistics, is copied to our server so that
the link can be opened in a browser. That grants us the limited right to store
and display that content for the purpose of serving the link, and nothing else.

A share link is a **capability URL**: it carries no password, and anyone who
has the link can see the content, including where the track starts and ends.
Treat a link as public. Do not share a ride that starts at your home address
unless you are content for that to be visible.

A shared item is deleted automatically **one year** after it was created.
There is currently no way to withdraw a link before then from inside the app,
so treat a link you hand out as public for that year; write to us if one has to
come down sooner.

If a share link points at content that infringes your rights or exposes your
personal data, write to **hello@orkitec.com** with the link itself and what the
problem is. We have no account to look the content up by, so the link is what we
need. We remove content that is clearly unlawful or infringing, usually within
a few working days.

## 7. The assistant

The assistant is off until you enable it, and it asks for your consent before
anything leaves the phone. What is sent is described in the
[privacy policy](./privacy): your text, optionally a starting position rounded
to about a kilometre, your language and unit settings, and, for a route
description, a short summary of the route.

The assistant runs on a large language model operated by a third party. Its
answers can be wrong, incomplete or nonsensical, and it may suggest a place
that does not exist or a distance it cannot deliver. Everything it returns is a
suggestion to be checked, not advice to be followed. The route itself is
computed on your phone, not by the model.

Fair use applies: the assistant is rate-limited, and heavy automated use may be
blocked. We may cap or pause the assistant if provider costs or abuse make that
necessary.

There is a **Report AI output** item in **Settings → AI assistant** for an
answer that was wrong or inappropriate, and we would like to hear about those.
